# Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Home Harvest Network (Pty) Ltd**
Last updated: 20 July 2026

This policy explains how subscription cancellations, missed deliveries, and quality issues are handled at Home Harvest Network. It is written in plain language and should be read together with our Terms and Conditions.

---

## 1. Overview

1.1 Because Home Harvest Network sells fresh, perishable produce rather than goods that can be returned and resold, this policy works a little differently to a typical retail returns policy. We've tried to make it fair, clear, and generous where it matters most — the quality of what actually arrives at your door.

1.2 In short:
- You can cancel your ongoing **subscription** at any time.
- We cannot accept the **return of produce that has already been delivered**, for the reasons explained in clause 3.
- If something is **wrong with your Box** — poor quality, missing items, or damaged produce — we will make it right. See clause 4.

---

## 2. Cancelling Your Subscription

2.1 You may cancel your Box subscription at any time by contacting us via WhatsApp, email, or through your account on our website.

2.2 To avoid being charged for the next billing cycle, please cancel at least **3 business days before your next scheduled payment date**. If you cancel after this point, your subscription will remain active (and your next Box will still be delivered) for the cycle you have already paid for, and will then stop.

2.3 There is no minimum subscription term and no cancellation fee for consumer subscriptions.

2.4 Business Client standing orders are governed by the notice period agreed in your individual supply agreement, which may differ from clause 2.2 above.

---

## 3. Our Policy on Returns of Delivered Produce

3.1 Home Harvest Network's products are consumable, perishable fresh produce. Under South African law (including the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act), consumable goods of this nature are treated differently from most other online purchases, and are excluded from the standard cooling-off right to return goods for any reason after delivery.

3.2 In practice, this means that once a Box has been successfully delivered to you, we are unable to accept its physical return or offer a refund simply because you changed your mind. This is both a legal reality for perishable food products and a practical one — produce that has left our care cannot be safely resold.

3.3 This does **not** affect your right to cancel your ongoing subscription (clause 2) or your right to a remedy for produce that does not meet the quality standard described in clause 4.

---

## 4. Quality Guarantee

4.1 We want every Box to meet a good standard of freshness and quality. If any part of your delivery does not — for example, produce that has arrived damaged, spoiled, significantly under-ripe or over-ripe beyond normal variation, or an item that was missing from your Box — please tell us within **24 hours of delivery**.

4.2 To report an issue, message us on WhatsApp or email with:
- Your name and delivery address
- A short description of the issue
- A photo of the affected produce, where possible

4.3 Once we've confirmed the issue, we will offer one of the following, at your choice where practical:
- **Replacement** of the affected item(s) in your next delivery, at no extra cost;
- **Account credit** applied to your next billing cycle; or
- **Partial or full refund** to your original payment method, depending on the extent of the issue.

4.4 Refunds under this clause are processed within **7 business days** of the issue being confirmed.

4.5 This quality guarantee reflects your rights under the Consumer Protection Act to receive goods that are safe, of good quality, and fit for their intended purpose. Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under that Act.

---

## 5. Missed or Failed Deliveries

5.1 If a scheduled delivery does not arrive on the expected day through our fault (for example, a missed collection, route, or packing error), please let us know within 24 hours. We will arrange a replacement delivery as soon as possible, or apply a credit to your account, at our discretion.

5.2 If a delivery cannot be completed because of incorrect address information, no safe/accessible place to leave the delivery, or unavailability at the agreed time despite reasonable delivery attempts, this will not be treated as a missed delivery for purposes of clause 5.1, and standard billing will continue to apply.

---

## 6. Failed or Missing Payment

6.1 If your scheduled subscription payment fails or is not received, we will notify you and give you an opportunity to make payment. If payment is still not received, your next delivery may be paused until payment is successfully made, and your subscription slot may be offered to another subscriber on our waitlist.

6.2 No refund is due for a delivery that did not occur because payment was not received.

---

## 7. How Refunds Are Paid

7.1 Where a refund is due under this policy, it will be paid back to the original payment method used (EFT or SnapScan, as applicable), within the timeframe stated in the relevant clause above, unless you and Home Harvest Network agree otherwise (for example, an account credit instead of a cash refund).

---

## 8. Business (B2B) Clients

8.1 Refunds, credits, and cancellation terms for Business Client standing orders are governed by the individual supply agreement in place between Home Harvest Network and the Business Client. Where that agreement is silent on a matter, the relevant principles in this policy will apply as a reasonable default.
