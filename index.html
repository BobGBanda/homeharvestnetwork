How It Works

Simple. Local. Powerful.

We connect unused residential backyards, trained growers, and hungry households into one self-sustaining urban food network.

01
🏡

Homeowners offer their yard

Households with unused space sign a simple agreement. We assess, prepare, and install irrigation at no cost to them.

02
🌱

We plant and manage everything

Trained plot managers handle soil prep, planting, watering, pest control, and harvesting. Homeowners do nothing.

03
📦

Produce is packed and delivered

Harvested veg is quality-checked, QR-tagged, packed in branded kraft packaging, and delivered to subscribers and buyers.

04
💵

Farmers earn passive income

Monthly flat fee plus revenue share, paid directly. Plus a weekly fresh produce allocation from their own plot.

05
📱

You track every step

From your account dashboard, see your box contents, delivery status, and scan the QR code to meet your farmer.

06
🌍

The city becomes food secure

Distributed urban growing reduces food miles, supports local jobs, and builds community resilience across KZN.
