How it works

We coordinate local growers, organise harvests, and deliver fresh produce directly to households and businesses across KwaZulu-Natal.

1

Local growers cultivate

Backyard growers produce fresh seasonal crops using available space within their communities.

2

Home Harvest Network coordinates supply

We organise crop planning, quality checks, packaging, and collection from growers.

3

Fresh produce delivered

Produce is distributed to households, restaurants, schools, and independent businesses.
