1. Who we are

Home Harvest Network ("we", "us", "our") connects local growers with households and businesses across KwaZulu-Natal and the Western Cape, and coordinates the collection, packaging, and delivery of fresh produce boxes. By subscribing to a produce box or otherwise using our services, you agree to these Terms & Conditions.

2. Subscriptions and billing

When you subscribe, you choose a box size (Mini, Family, or Premium), a delivery frequency (weekly or monthly), and a billing method:

  • Recurring billing: you authorise us, via our payment processor PayFast, to automatically charge your selected card at the start of each delivery cycle until you cancel.
  • Pay-per-delivery: you approve and pay for each delivery individually before it is dispatched.

Prices are shown in South African Rand (ZAR) and may be updated from time to time. We will give reasonable notice before any price change takes effect on an active subscription.

3. Payments

All online payments are processed securely by PayFast, a licensed South African payment gateway. We do not store your card details. Payments can be made by Visa or Mastercard debit or credit card. We do not have access to your banking credentials at any point in this process.

4. Cancellations and pauses

You may cancel or pause a recurring subscription at any time by contacting us before your next billing date; changes take effect from the next delivery cycle. Once a delivery cycle has been charged and produce has been sourced on your behalf, that cycle is non-refundable, except as set out below.

5. Refunds

If a delivery is missed, significantly delayed, or arrives with produce that does not meet reasonable quality expectations, contact us within 48 hours of the scheduled delivery and we will offer a replacement, credit toward your next box, or a refund for that delivery, at our discretion.

6. Delivery

Delivery is currently available within our supported KwaZulu-Natal and Western Cape delivery areas. We will confirm whether your suburb is covered when you subscribe. Delivery days and times may vary slightly depending on harvest schedules and grower availability.

7. Produce sourcing

Produce is sourced seasonally from local growers within our network. Exact contents of each box may vary week to week depending on availability, weather, and harvest conditions. Substitutions of similar value may be made where a specific item is unavailable.

8. For Growers and For Business

Separate arrangements apply to growers supplying produce through our network and to businesses (restaurants, schools, corporate canteens) receiving standing orders. These are agreed individually and are not covered by the household subscription terms above.
