3. About our service

Home Harvest Network coordinates a network of independent local growers to supply fresh, seasonal fruit and vegetables to households (via recurring subscription boxes) and businesses (via standing-order supply arrangements).

We currently offer three consumer subscription tiers — Mini Box, Family Box, and Premium Box — each with different produce quantities and pricing, delivered weekly or bi-weekly depending on your selection. Current tier contents and pricing are published on our Subscriptions page and may be updated from time to time in accordance with section 17.

We are an aggregator and distributor of produce grown by our Grower network. We do not grow produce ourselves, and the specific mix of items in any Box will vary according to seasonal availability.

9.1 Overview

Because Home Harvest Network sells fresh, perishable produce rather than goods that can be returned and resold, this policy works a little differently to a typical retail returns policy. We've tried to make it fair, clear, and generous where it matters most — the quality of what actually arrives at your door.

In short:

  • You can cancel your ongoing subscription at any time.
  • We cannot accept the return of produce that has already been delivered, for the reasons explained in section 9.3.
  • If something is wrong with your Box — poor quality, missing items, or damaged produce — we will make it right. See section 9.4.

9.2 Cancelling your subscription

You may cancel your Box subscription at any time by contacting us via WhatsApp, email, or through your account on our website.

To avoid being charged for the next billing cycle, please cancel at least 3 business days before your next scheduled payment date. If you cancel after this point, your subscription will remain active (and your next Box will still be delivered) for the cycle you have already paid for, and will then stop.

There is no minimum subscription term and no cancellation fee for consumer subscriptions.

Business Client standing orders are governed by the notice period agreed in your individual supply agreement, which may differ from the consumer notice period above.

9.3 Our policy on returns of delivered produce

Home Harvest Network's products are consumable, perishable fresh produce. Under South African law (including the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act), consumable goods of this nature are treated differently from most other online purchases, and are excluded from the standard cooling-off right to return goods for any reason after delivery.

In practice, this means that once a Box has been successfully delivered to you, we are unable to accept its physical return or offer a refund simply because you changed your mind. This is both a legal reality for perishable food products and a practical one — produce that has left our care cannot be safely resold.

This does not affect your right to cancel your ongoing subscription (section 9.2) or your right to a remedy for produce that does not meet the quality standard described in section 9.4.

9.4 Quality guarantee

We want every Box to meet a good standard of freshness and quality. If any part of your delivery does not — for example, produce that has arrived damaged, spoiled, significantly under-ripe or over-ripe beyond normal variation, or an item that was missing from your Box — please tell us within 24 hours of delivery.

To report an issue, message us on WhatsApp or email with:

  • Your name and delivery address
  • A short description of the issue
  • A photo of the affected produce, where possible

Once we've confirmed the issue, we will offer one of the following, at your choice where practical:

  • Replacement of the affected item(s) in your next delivery, at no extra cost;
  • Account credit applied to your next billing cycle; or
  • Partial or full refund to your original payment method, depending on the extent of the issue.

Refunds under this section are processed within 7 business days of the issue being confirmed.

This quality guarantee reflects your rights under the CPA to receive goods that are safe, of good quality, and fit for their intended purpose. Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under that Act.

If a delivery cannot be completed because of incorrect address information, no safe or accessible place to leave the delivery, or unavailability at the agreed time despite reasonable delivery attempts, this will not be treated as a missed delivery for purposes of section 9.5, and standard billing will continue to apply.

9.6 Failed or missing payment

If payment is still not received, your next delivery may be paused until payment is successfully made, and your subscription slot may be offered to another subscriber on our waitlist.

No refund is due for a delivery that did not occur because payment was not received.
